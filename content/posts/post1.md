The problem doesn't lie in identifying slop. It is still possible, sometime even blatantly obvious, and yet, we yield, mostly because it's slop baked on top of things you _actually_ like. Take me, for example. Anyone who knows me well knows of the vice I share with every second 16 year old - the trope of the bad boy with a heart of gold. [https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Main/JerkWithAHeartOfGold]. For the past 8 years, I have devoured and drained most novels, movies and fanfictions of this trope. Now if you show me an ad for one of those vertical reel-web-dramas about a mafia eyecandy who secretly loathes the underworld but wouldn't go against it except for the girl he loves, I would watch it in a heartbeat, knowing that it's AI slop. Would I have to bury my self-respect and arrange it's funeral while paying for coins to unlock the climax episodes? Absolutely. Would I do it anyway? Without a doubt.


What you end up with is content that is not made for you. It is made against you — engineered to hold your eyes open at the lowest possible cost to the operator and an unaccounted cost to your attention. The product is whatever keeps you watching.
